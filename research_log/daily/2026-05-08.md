**Walk-forward (truncated 2020-03 → 2024-12, 1,767 bars):**

| Config | Sharpe | Return | MaxDD |
|---|---|---|---|

ΔSharpe (scaled) = **+0.181** — but on a much weaker truncated baseline,

**Late-cycle (Fold 2 = Oct 2021 → Jul 2022, the H7 target):**
- V2 truncated bull IC: **+0.1065**
- V4 bull IC: **−0.0106**
- Δ bull IC: **−0.117** (worse, not better)

**Pre-committed decision rule fires REJECT:** no bull-IC improvement at
the H7 target. Aggregate Sharpe lift of +0.181 is real but cosmetic for
the hypothesis (comes from non-target Folds 3–4).

**Accidental discovery — H8.** V2 truncated has Fold-2 bull IC of +0.1065
vs V2 6yr's −0.128. **+0.235 swing purely from removing the 2019 / early-2020
training bars.** This means the original Fold-2 ATH+crash failure
(learning #5, F14, motivation for both P-ML15 and P-ML19) was a
*training-data* problem, not a missing-signal problem. Adding funding
rate on top of the now-good V2 truncated bull model actively *hurts* it
(funding rate is a weak signal that disrupts a working setup).

Full writeup in F24 (findings.md).

## §2 — P-ML20 Part 1 (Sliding-start ablation, same session)

### Work Done
- Built `notebooks/p_ml20_sample_window.ipynb` (19 cells). Strategy:
  hold V2 6yr outer 5-fold WF schedule fixed; vary the training mask by
  cutoff date so per-fold metrics are directly comparable.
- Cutoffs tested: 2019-03 (full V2 baseline), 2019-06, 2019-09, 2020-01,
  2020-03, 2020-06.
- Executed end-to-end via nbconvert in ~3 min.

### Findings (F25)

**Mechanical observation.** V2 rolling 533-bar training windows for
Folds 3–5 begin after 2020-08, so cutoffs ≤ 2020-06 leave their training
identical → byte-identical Fold-3/4/5 ICs across all cutoffs. The
experiment is effectively a Fold-1 / Fold-2 sweep with Folds 3–5 held
fixed.

**Aggregate Sharpe (scaled mode):**

| Cutoff | #folds | Sharpe | ΔSharpe |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2019-03 (baseline) | 5/5 | +1.583 | — |
| 2019-06 | 5/5 | +1.610 | +0.027 |
| 2019-09 | 5/5 | +1.661 | +0.077 |
| 2020-01 | 4/5 | +1.410 | −0.173 |
| 2020-03 | 4/5 | +1.763 | +0.180 |
| **2020-06** | **4/5** | **+1.679** | **+0.096** |

**Fold-2 bull IC (the H8 target — apples-to-apples since test bars fixed):**

| Cutoff | F2 Bull IC | Δ |
|---|---|---|
| 2019-03 (baseline) | −0.128 | — |
| 2019-06 | −0.128 | 0.000 |
| 2019-09 | −0.128 | 0.000 |
| 2020-01 | −0.126 | +0.002 |
| **2020-03** | **−0.162** | **−0.034** |
| **2020-06** | **−0.023** | **+0.105** |

**Key insight: the COVID shock (Mar–May 2020) is the bug.** Cutoff 2020-03
keeps the COVID shock in training and produces the WORST Fold-2 bull IC.
Cutoff 2020-06 drops it entirely and produces a near-neutral Fold-2 bull
IC. Pre-COVID 2019 data is essentially inert (only 42 bull bars; cutoffs
2019-06 / 2019-09 don't change Fold-2 IC at all).

**Discovered**: H8's original framing ("pre-2020 data hurts") was too
broad. Refined: **only the Mar–May 2020 window (COVID crash + recovery
rally) hurts the bull model**.

**Connection to F24.** P-ML19's V2-truncated dataset showed Fold-2 bull
IC of +0.107 at start 2020-03. P-ML20's cutoff 2020-03 (same start date,
fixed outer schedule) shows Fold-2 bull IC of −0.162. The +0.270
discrepancy is the *fold-boundary artefact* — truncating the dataset
shifts the calendar window of "Fold 2" (Oct 2021 → Jul 2022 in P-ML19 vs
Feb 2021 → Jan 2022 here), and the bull model behaves very differently on
those different test bars. So F24's apparent +0.235 swing was mostly
fold-boundary, with only a small genuine training-data effect at the same
start date.

**Caveat.** Cutoff variants ≥ 2020-01 skip Fold 1 (training too small
after dropping pre-2020). The 4-fold Sharpe (+1.679 for cutoff 2020-06)
covers Folds 2–5 (2021-02 → 2024-12), while the 5-fold baseline (+1.583)
covers Folds 1–5 (2020-02 → 2024-12). The Fold-2 bull-IC swing is
rigorous; the Sharpe swing is suggestive but contaminated.

**Verdict.** Pre-committed strict-win rule fires for cutoff 2020-06-01.
But the Sharpe-comparison caveat means we should validate via sample
weighting (Part 2) before formally promoting to champion. Marking as
**champion candidate** in the roadmap.
