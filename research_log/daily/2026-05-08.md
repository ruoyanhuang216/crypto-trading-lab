## Findings

### F24 — H7 rejected; accidental discovery about 2019 training data

**IC screen (3 candidates, 1 passed):**

| Feature | IC_total | IC_bull | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| funding_3d_mean | −0.013 | −0.007 | no |
| funding_zscore_30d | −0.034 | +0.036 | YES |
| funding_persistence_3d | −0.008 | +0.008 | no |

None statistically significant (all p > 0.15). **FEATURES_V4 = V2 +
funding_zscore_30d (17 features).**

**Walk-forward (truncated 2020-03 → 2024-12, 1,767 bars):**

| Config | Sharpe | Return | MaxDD |
|---|---|---|---|
| V2 truncated scaled (re-baseline) | +0.594 | +74.5% | −42.8% |
| V4 (V2 + funding) scaled | +0.775 | +123.1% | −42.2% |
| V2 truncated binary | +0.491 | +56.6% | −73.2% |
| V4 binary | +0.090 | −42.8% | −71.4% |

ΔSharpe (scaled) = **+0.181** — but on a much weaker truncated baseline,
not the V2 6yr champion (+1.583).

**Late-cycle (Fold 2 = Oct 2021 → Jul 2022, the H7 target):**
- V2 truncated bull IC: **+0.1065**
- V4 bull IC: **−0.0106**
- Δ bull IC: **−0.117** (worse, not better)

**Pre-committed decision rule fires REJECT:** no bull-IC improvement at
the H7 target. Aggregate Sharpe lift of +0.181 is real but cosmetic for
the hypothesis (comes from non-target Folds 3–4).

**Accidental discovery — H8.** V2 truncated has Fold-2 bull IC of +0.1065
vs V2 6yr's −0.128. **+0.235 swing purely from removing the 2019 / early-2020
training bars.** This means the original Fold-2 ATH+crash failure
(learning #5, F14, motivation for both P-ML15 and P-ML19) was a
*training-data* problem, not a missing-signal problem. Adding funding
rate on top of the now-good V2 truncated bull model actively *hurts* it
(funding rate is a weak signal that disrupts a working setup).

Full writeup in F24 (findings.md).

## Open Questions

- **Why does pre-2020 BTC training data hurt the bull model?** Candidate
  explanations: (a) 2019 was a recovery-from-2018-bear regime that's
  qualitatively different from the 2020-2021 ETF/COVID-driven bull;
  (b) early-2020 COVID shock was a black-swan that biases the bull model
  toward over-reacting to subsequent runs; (c) BTC's microstructure
  changed materially in 2020 with institutional adoption (per F19/F20
  cross-asset findings). P-ML20 will probe this.
- **Does the +0.181 V4 scaled Sharpe lift reproduce on V2 6yr?** Can't
  test directly because funding-rate data is not available pre-2020.
  If we trust the lift, the V4 6yr Sharpe might land around +1.583+0.18 =
  +1.76. But the lift comes from non-target folds, so this is speculative.
- **Should V2 6yr remain champion?** P-ML20 will answer this. If V2-truncated
  strictly dominates V2 6yr on the matched 2020+ window, the champion baseline
  shifts and many downstream experiments need re-baselining.
