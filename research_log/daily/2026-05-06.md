## Findings

### F23 — Optuna tuning rejected (full writeup in `findings.md`)

**Inner-CV Sharpe +1.6119, outer-WF Sharpe +0.980 vs defaults +1.583
(Δ −0.604).** 35 of 100 trials completed, 65 pruned by MedianPruner.

| Param | Tuned | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `n_estimators` | 450 | 300 |
| `max_depth` | **8** *(top of range)* | 4 |
| `learning_rate` | 0.01171 | 0.05 |
| `min_child_samples` | **49** *(top of range)* | 20 |
| `reg_alpha` | 0.51408 | 0.0 |
| `reg_lambda` | 0.00340 | 0.0 |
| `pred_zscore_window` | 50 | 60 |
| `position_scale` | 0.46268 | 0.50 |

**Lift = −0.604 → triggers "lift < +0.05" decision-rule branch: P-ML15 DONE.**

Two root causes diagnosed:

1. **Inner-CV overfit.** 3-fold inner Sharpe (+1.61) overstated outer 5-fold
   Sharpe (+0.98) by +0.63 — the signature of TPE selection bias in a 7-dim
   space at 100 trials.
2. **Bull-model fragility.** Tuned `min_child_samples=49` on ~130 bull bars/fold
   makes the bull sub-model degenerate (Bull IC = nan in every outer fold,
   vs defaults bull IC +0.058 to +0.071 in folds 4–5). Inner CV's fold mix
   hid the failure mode.

Per-fold tuned IC: +0.072 → −0.070 (Fold 1), wiping out the outer mean.
ICIR collapses from +1.779 to +0.522. Hand-picked defaults aren't just
near-optimal — they're actively better than what TPE found.
