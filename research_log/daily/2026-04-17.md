## Design decisions (agreed)

**Scale grounding:** 6yr daily ≈ 2,171 bars. RegimeEnsemble fits 2 LGBM
models per trial ≈ 5s wall time including predict + equity build.

### Search space — Medium (7 dims)

LightGBM: `n_estimators`, `max_depth`, `learning_rate`, `min_child_samples`,
`reg_alpha`, `reg_lambda`. Positioning: `pred_zscore_window`, `position_scale`.

Rejected dims:
- `subsample` / `colsample_bytree`: low-value on tabular data <2k rows.
- `num_leaves`: redundant with `max_depth`.

Expected lift: +0.10–0.25 Sharpe at ~60–80 trials.

### CV structure — Shared-study 3-fold inner CV

- One Optuna study; each trial evaluated by mean Sharpe across 3 inner
  purged folds on the full 6yr dataset.
- Outer 5-fold walk-forward is preserved for final reporting — Optuna
  never sees outer test bars.
- Rejected: single train/val split (2019–23 → 2024) — 2024 is bull-heavy,
  would overfit tuning to bull regime (F10 already flagged bull model as
  the fragile sub-model).
- Rejected: nested per-outer-fold (5 × 3 × 100 = 1,500 fits, ~2 hr) —
  over-rigorous; outer Sharpe variance already surfaces param instability.

Wall time: ~25 min (300 fits at 5s).

### Objective — Sharpe on OOS equity

Rejected ICIR: P-ML8 (F15) proved IC screens can pass while ensemble
Sharpe collapses. IC misses the positioning × model interaction that
drives V2-scaled's edge.

Rejected Calmar (Sharpe / |MaxDD|): double-counts risk; Sharpe is already
vol-adjusted. Over-rewards low-DD folds idiosyncratically.

### Budget

100 trials with TPE sampler + MedianPruner. Total ~25 min.

### Target

Tune V2-24/7 scaled first. If lift exceeds +0.15 Sharpe, re-apply the same
best params to V2-weekday as a sanity check. If lift <+0.05, P-ML15 is
done and we move to P-ML16 (expanding-window walk-forward).

## Appendix — Cost-benefit reasoning

Kept here so the design rationale survives the session. If a future
experiment revisits these knobs, start from this table rather than
re-deriving from scratch.

**Baseline cost unit:** one trial = one RegimeEnsemble fit (bull +
non-bull LGBM) + predict + positioning + equity build ≈ **5 s** on
2,171-bar × 16-feature dataset.

### Search-space dimensionality

| Option | Dims | Trials to converge | Wall (100 trials) | Expected Sharpe lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wide  | 11 | 100–150 | ~8 min | +0.15–0.30 |
| **Medium** | **7** | **60–80** | **~5 min** | **+0.10–0.25** |
| Narrow | 5 | 40–60 | ~3 min | +0.05–0.15 |

Reasoning: TPE sampler efficiency degrades with dimensionality; rule of
thumb ~10× dim for decent coverage. The wide space's extra dims
(`subsample`, `colsample_bytree`, `num_leaves`) are typically low-value
on tabular data with <2k rows. `num_leaves` is partially redundant with
`max_depth`. Medium captures the high-value axes (boosting rounds via
`n_estimators`/`lr`, capacity via `max_depth`, regularisation via
`min_child_samples`/`reg_α`/`reg_λ`, and the positioning interaction).

### CV structure

| Option | Fits | Wall time | Robustness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single split (2019–23 → 2024) | 100 | ~8 min | Low — 2024 is bull-heavy, overfits to one regime |
| **Shared-study 3-fold inner CV** | **300** | **~25 min** | **High — regime diversity, single TPE history** |
| Nested per-outer-fold (5 × 3 × 100) | 1,500 | ~2 hr | Overkill — outer Sharpe variance already surfaces instability |

Reasoning: single-split is 3× faster but the validation period decides
the optimum, and 2024 is bull-heavy. F10 already flagged the bull
sub-model as the fragile one, so biasing tuning toward a bull regime
risks picking params that won't generalise. Shared-study keeps the TPE
history unified (each trial scored by mean across 3 folds), so TPE still
learns efficiently — unlike nested per-outer-fold which runs 5 separate
studies and would need 5× the budget to match the learning signal.

### Objective

| Option | Overhead | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| ICIR | ~0% | P-ML8 (F15) proved IC screens pass while Sharpe collapses; blind to positioning |
| **Sharpe on OOS equity** | **+15%** | **Matches ranking metric; captures position × model interaction** |
| Calmar (Sharpe / |MaxDD|) | +15% | Double-counts risk (Sharpe is already vol-adjusted); over-rewards idiosyncratic low-DD folds |

Reasoning: ICIR is tempting because it skips equity simulation, but
V2-scaled's edge over V2-binary (+1.583 vs +1.234) is entirely
positioning — IC cannot see that. Calmar sounds risk-aware but
empirically produces noisy rankings: MaxDD depends heavily on fold
boundaries and a single bad day can swing Calmar much more than Sharpe.

### Final choice

**Medium search space × shared-study 3-fold CV × Sharpe objective ≈ 25 min
wall time, expected +0.10–0.25 Sharpe lift.**

Decision rule after first run:
- Lift > +0.15 → second pass with wide search space (diminishing returns
  probably kicks in, but worth checking).
- Lift +0.05 to +0.15 → freeze best params, ship to V2-weekday, move to P-ML16.
- Lift < +0.05 → P-ML15 done, move to P-ML16. Hand-picked defaults were
  near-optimal (which is itself a useful finding — means remaining alpha
  is not in the hyperparameter surface).
